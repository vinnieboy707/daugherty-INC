## Scheduling Process

### Step 1: Initial Consultation Request

To schedule a consultation with Nate, please provide the following information:

#### Required Information

1. **Personal Details:**
   - Full Name
   - Phone Number
   - Email Address
   - Best Time to Contact You

2. **Tattoo Details:**
   - **Description of the Tattoo:** Provide a detailed description of your tattoo idea
   - **Background Story:** Share the meaning and story behind your tattoo
   - **Desired Placement:** Specify where on your body you'd like the tattoo
   - **Pain Threshold:** Let us know your comfort level with pain (Low, Medium, High)

3. **Special Requests (Optional):**
   - VIP Treatment Options (see below)

### Step 2: Consultation

Nate offers consultations to discuss your tattoo vision:

- **Phone Consultation:** Discuss your ideas, placement, and design over the phone
- **In-Person Consultation:** Meet with Nate to review designs, verify placement, and finalize details

During the consultation, Nate will:
- Review your tattoo concept
- Verify the placement on your body
- Discuss design modifications if needed
- Lock in your appointment date and time

### Step 3: Appointment Confirmation

After your consultation, your appointment will be confirmed and scheduled.
