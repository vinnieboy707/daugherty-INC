Scheduling Process

Step 1: Initial Consultation Request

To schedule a consultation with Nate, please provide the following information:

Required Information

  1. Personal Details:
    • Full Name
    • Phone Number
    • Email Address
    • Best Time to Contact You
  2. Tattoo Details:
    • Description of the Tattoo: Provide a detailed description of your tattoo idea
    • Background Story: Share the meaning and story behind your tattoo
    • Desired Placement: Specify where on your body you'd like the tattoo
    • Pain Threshold: Let us know your comfort level with pain (Low, Medium, High)
  3. Special Requests (Optional):
    • VIP Treatment Options (see below)

Step 2: Consultation

Nate offers consultations to discuss your tattoo vision:

  • Phone Consultation: Discuss your ideas, placement, and design over the phone
  • In-Person Consultation: Meet with Nate to review designs, verify placement, and finalize details

During the consultation, Nate will:

  • Review your tattoo concept
  • Verify the placement on your body
  • Discuss design modifications if needed
  • Lock in your appointment date and time

Step 3: Appointment Confirmation

After your consultation, your appointment will be confirmed and scheduled.

VIP Treatment Options

For clients with special needs or time constraints, Nate offers VIP treatment services for an additional fee:

VIP Services Include:

  1. Priority Scheduling
    • Meet specific deadlines to get your tattoo completed by a certain date
    • Expedited appointment booking
  2. Home Visit Service
    • Nate can come to your location for the tattoo session
    • Perfect for those with mobility issues or privacy preferences
  3. Travel Services
    • Nate is available for travel to your city/location
    • Ideal for destination tattoos or special events
  4. Extended Sessions
    • Longer, uninterrupted sessions to complete larger pieces
Note: VIP services require advance booking and additional fees. Please inquire during your consultation for pricing and availability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does the consultation take?

A: Consultations typically last 15-30 minutes for phone consultations and 30-60 minutes for in-person consultations.

Q: Is there a consultation fee?

A: [To be determined - consult with Nate]

Q: How far in advance should I book?

A: Booking times vary based on Nate's schedule. We recommend reaching out at least 2-4 weeks in advance for regular appointments. VIP services may require additional lead time.

Q: What should I bring to an in-person consultation?

A: Bring any reference images, design ideas, and be ready to discuss placement on your body.

Q: Can I make changes after the consultation?

A: Minor changes can often be accommodated. Significant changes may require an additional consultation.
